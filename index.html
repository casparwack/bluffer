    How to play

    Bet big.
    Back it up.

    1. 1
      Place your bet

      You get one category a day. Bet how many you think you can name.

    2. 2
      Name them

      20 seconds per answer. The clock resets every time you land one. Wrong guesses don't reset it, so don't panic-type.

    3. 3
      Cash out

      Every answer drops a bundle of cash on the table. Reach your bet line and you bank it. Fall short and you're busted.

    The money

    Every answer in your bet is worth 150, win or lose.

    Each answer you back up+150
    Each extra past your bet+25
    Each one you're short−150

    Bust and you get nothing for the ones you did name. Only the misses count.

    Bet smart

    Say you can actually name 8.

    Bet low and you leave money on the table. Bet high and you pay for every miss. The best score is betting exactly what you can back up.
